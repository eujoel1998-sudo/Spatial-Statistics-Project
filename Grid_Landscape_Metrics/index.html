01 — Why Look at the Grid

Regime Averages Hide Real Within-District Spread

South City's regime-level Patch Density in 2015 was a single number: 17.49. At grid resolution, the cells that make up South City range from 3.96 to over 2,000 — most of that range compressed into a modest, informative spread, with a handful of extreme values coming from tiny boundary-sliver cells where a small denominator inflates a density metric (a classic modifiable-areal-unit effect, not a real fragmentation signal). The interactive map's quantile-based color scale is robust to those outliers; hovering an individual cell shows its exact value regardless.

The citywide mean confirms the same story the regime view told — fragmentation rising through ~2015–2020, then predicted to consolidate — but every cell behind that mean is now individually inspectable on the map, rather than folded into one of five regime numbers.
